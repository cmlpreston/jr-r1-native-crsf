Steering & throttle

W / S: forward / brake · A / D: steer while held.

About this demo

The LCD is rendered by the current C++/LVGL interface. Each orientation has its own saved demo settings. This does not connect to radio hardware or validate real RF timing, receiver behaviour or failsafe operation.
